Adwa is a town in central Tigray surrounded by distinctive mountains. It is known internationally for the Battle of Adwa, fought on 1 March 1896, in which Ethiopian forces led by Emperor Menelik II defeated an invading Italian army.

The victory secured Ethiopia's independence during the colonial period and is commemorated every year as Adwa Victory Day, which falls on Yekatit 23 in the Ethiopian calendar.
