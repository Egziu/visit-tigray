Mekelle is the capital of the Tigray region. In the 1880s Emperor Yohannes IV built a palace in Mekelle, which later became a museum.

Opening hours, entry arrangements and the current status of museums and cultural venues must be confirmed with the Tourism Bureau before visiting.
