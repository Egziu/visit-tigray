Meskel celebrates the Finding of the True Cross. It falls on Meskerem 17 in the Ethiopian calendar (27 September in the Gregorian calendar, or 28 September in years that follow an Ethiopian leap year) and is marked with large bonfires called Demera.

Timket, the Ethiopian Orthodox celebration of Epiphany, falls on Tir 11 (19 January, or 20 January in Gregorian leap years). Processions carry replicas of the tabot.

Ashenda is a festival celebrated in Tigray mainly by girls and young women in August, at the end of the Filseta fast, with songs, dancing and braided hairstyles.

The Ethiopian New Year, Enkutatash, falls on Meskerem 1 (11 September, or 12 September in the year after an Ethiopian leap year).
