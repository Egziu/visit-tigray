The Gheralta mountains near Hawzen are known for sandstone cliffs and for rock-hewn churches carved into the rock, several of them high on cliff faces. Abuna Yemata Guh is a cliff church whose approach involves a steep climb and is not suitable for everyone.

Tigray has many rock-hewn churches; published counts vary and are often given as more than one hundred. The dating of individual churches is debated among scholars, and local tradition and academic estimates may differ.

Many of the churches contain wall paintings that are fragile. Access often depends on the presence of a priest who holds the key, and climbing routes may require experience and a qualified local guide. Conditions and permissions must be confirmed locally.
