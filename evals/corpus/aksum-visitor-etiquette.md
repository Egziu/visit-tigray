Churches and monasteries in Tigray are active places of worship. Visitors are generally asked to remove their shoes before entering a church, to dress modestly with shoulders and knees covered, and to speak quietly.

Photography rules are set by each church's custodians. Always ask before taking photographs, and never photograph worshippers, priests or sacred objects without explicit permission. Flash photography can damage ancient paintings.

Some monasteries do not admit women; one well-known example is Debre Damo, a monastery on a flat-topped mountain that is reached by climbing a rope. Follow the instructions of custodians and local guides at every site.

Access arrangements, entry contributions and opening times are decided locally and change; confirm them with the Tourism Bureau or a licensed local guide before visiting.
