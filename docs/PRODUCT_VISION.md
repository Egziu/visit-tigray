# Product Vision — VISIT TIGRAY 2.0

**Discover. Experience. Remember.**

Visit Tigray aims to be a regional digital tourism operating system: a public window onto Tigray's heritage, an intelligent and
honest travel companion, a community memory of journeys, and a dependable management tool for the Tigrai Culture and Tourism
Bureau. SERK Technologies Group is the technology partner. The platform becomes an official service only with the Bureau's written
authorization.

## Who it serves

| Audience | Need | What the platform offers |
|---|---|---|
| International visitors | Inspiration and trustworthy preparation | Editorial destinations, cited AI guide, deterministic planner, advisories, offline access |
| Diaspora | Connection to heritage and language | Tigrinya-first content, ancestral and heritage themes, memories |
| Scholars and students | Sourced knowledge | Knowledge Vault citations, timelines, contested claims shown with sources |
| Local providers | Visibility and trust | Verified directory, inquiries (reservations later, after approvals) |
| Communities and custodians | Respect and control | Custodian approval for sacred content, protected coordinates, takedown |
| The Bureau | Accurate content, insight, accountability | CMS with workflow, analytics with provenance, audit, an AI assistant that cannot act on its own |

## Differentiators (from the global benchmark, `docs/research/GLOBAL_BENCHMARK.md`)

1. **Every AI answer is cited**, and the guide says "I don't know" rather than guess. None of the benchmarked national tourism
   assistants were observed citing sources per answer.
2. **Heritage stewardship by design**: coarsened coordinates for protected sites, custodian approvals, and contested history attributed
   rather than resolved.
3. **Tigrinya, Amharic and English**, with Ethiopic-aware search, the Ethiopian calendar, and human review of every translation.
4. **Local-first AI and offline resilience** for low-connectivity contexts: retrieval works without a model and without the internet.
5. **Honest operations**: unknown access stays unknown; advisories disable routing; no fabricated prices, hours or availability.

## Non-goals (for now)

* Payments and bookings (designed, flag-gated, off).
* Social-media style engagement optimization or behavioural advertising.
* Presenting reconstructions or AI imagery as authentic photographs.
